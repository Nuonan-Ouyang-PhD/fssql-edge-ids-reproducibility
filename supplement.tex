\section{Additional Experimental Details}
This supplement provides additional calibration, per-seed statistical, online deployment, energy, and test-set results for ``Shielded Q-learning for Thermally-Safe, Energy-Aware Multi-Model Intrusion Detection Scheduling on Lightweight Edge Devices.'' Calibration, Q training, online hardware experiments, physical-energy measurement, and statistical analysis were completed before the official UNSW-NB15 test was accessed. The test set was then evaluated once using fixed preprocessing and detector thresholds.

\section{Device-Specific Guard Calibration}
Both devices use independent fit, margin, and held-out coverage splits over 72 action/workload/background cells. Each split contains 2,880 windows. No coverage observation is used to refit a predictor or change a margin.

The Pi 4B guard is nonbinding in the accepted online conditions: the mean safe-set size is four for every policy/condition. Mean controller time across all conditions is 3.919 ms for FSSQL-R, 3.885 ms for Safe-Greedy, 4.277 ms for Static-Light, and 3.946 ms for Threshold. Mean selected-detector inference is 0.415, 0.426, 0.648, and 0.299 ms, respectively. This confirms that controller work is a material part of online cost.

The detector-selection confusion counts show why the Safe-Greedy comparison must be interpreted carefully: Safe-Greedy has FPR $=1.0000$ and TN $=0$ in all 15 Pi 4B cells, i.e., it is an all-positive lower-bound controller under the frozen action-utility rule. Mean FPR for FSSQL-R is 0.2936, 0.3060, and 0.3109 for B0, B1, and B2, respectively, whereas Threshold has 0.0726, 0.0581, and 0.0490. Thus the FSSQL-R--Safe-Greedy F1 difference partly represents recovery from a degenerate all-positive point, while Threshold recovers more F1 with substantially fewer false positives.

\section{Stateful Extension Stop Result}
A planned Pi 3B+ stateful-transition extension measured action-dependent service timing under B0/B1/B2, but the predefined service-model check could not simultaneously demonstrate backlog accumulation in B2/B1 and recovery in B0 without introducing a new arrival rate or changing the service criterion. The extension was therefore stopped before policy runs. This result does not provide evidence for or against Q-learning superiority.
